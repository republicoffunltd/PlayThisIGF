Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Republic of Fun LTD (“we”, “our”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use playthisigf.com or participate in the Festival.

2. Who We Are

Republic of Fun LTD • Dublin, Ireland • Email: info@republicoffunltd.com

3. Data We Collect

  • Contact data: name, email (newsletter, contact forms).
  • Event data: ticketing/registration via Eventbrite.
  • Analytics: anonymous usage metrics (Google Analytics).
  • Form submissions: Google Forms (e.g., volunteer/exhibit/newsletter).

4. How We Use Your Data

  • Send event updates and respond to enquiries.
  • Process ticketing and participation via Eventbrite.
  • Improve our website and communications.

We never sell or rent your personal information.

5. Lawful Basis

  • Consent — newsletter and form submissions.
  • Contract — ticket/registration processing.
  • Legitimate interest — site analytics and improvements.

You may withdraw consent anytime at info@republicoffunltd.com.

6. Sharing

We share limited data with trusted providers who act under their own policies:

  • Eventbrite (ticketing & registration)
  • Google (Analytics & Forms)

7. Retention

  • Newsletter/contact: until you unsubscribe.
  • Ticketing/registration: up to 12 months after the event.
  • Analytics: anonymised per Google’s policies.

8. Your Rights (GDPR)

Access, rectification, erasure, withdrawal of consent, and complaint to the Irish DPC (dataprotection.ie).

9. Security

We use HTTPS and reputable providers to protect your data.

10. Updates

We may update this policy. The latest version will always appear here.

11. Contact

Email: info@republicoffunltd.com
