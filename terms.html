We keep things friendly, fair, and clear. These Terms explain how Play This Indie Game Festival works so everyone can have a good time.

The Festival will take place at An Táin Arts Centre, Dundalk, Ireland, in April 2026. We may make reasonable changes to the schedule, venue layout, or participating speakers/exhibitors if necessary (for example, due to logistics or safety). We’ll communicate material changes as early as possible.

3. Tickets and Entry
