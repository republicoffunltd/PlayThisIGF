Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2025

Cookies help the site work smoothly (for example, ticket checkout and forms) and help us understand what’s useful to visitors.
Here’s a clear summary of what we use and how you stay in control.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that support website features and improve your browsing experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

  • Essential: site navigation and ticket checkout via Eventbrite.
  • Analytics: understand how visitors use our site (Google Analytics), with privacy-minded defaults.
  • Embedded content: Eventbrite widgets and Google Forms may set their own cookies.

3. Managing Cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect site functionality (for example, ticket checkout or form submissions).

4. Third-Party Cookies

  • Eventbrite — ticketing and registration.
  • Google Analytics — site usage analytics.
  • Google Forms — newsletter/sign-ups and submissions.

Please see each provider’s cookie/privacy policies for details.

5. Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy. Changes will be posted here with a new “Last updated” date.
