Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2025

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites work and improve your browsing experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

  • Essential: Site navigation, ticket checkout via Eventbrite.
  • Analytics: Understand how visitors use our site (Google Analytics).
  • Embedded content: Eventbrite widgets and Google Forms may set their own cookies.

3. Managing Cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect site functionality (e.g., ticket checkout or form submissions).

4. Third-Party Cookies

  • Eventbrite — ticketing and registration.
  • Google Analytics — site usage analytics.
  • Google Forms — newsletter/sign-ups and submissions.

Refer to each provider’s privacy/cookie policies for details.

5. Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy. Changes will be posted here with a new “Last updated” date.
